# ☕ Monday Coffee Expansion Analysis using SQL

## 📖 About the Project

Monday Coffee is an online coffee company planning to expand by opening three new coffee shops across India. This SQL project analyzes historical sales data to identify the best cities for expansion using business-driven insights.

The analysis focuses on revenue, customer behavior, product performance, rental costs, and market potential to recommend the top three cities for expansion.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Recommend the best three cities for opening new coffee shops based on:

- Total Revenue
- Customer Base
- Estimated Coffee Consumers
- Average Sales per Customer
- Average Rent per Customer
- Monthly Sales Growth
- Market Potential

---

## 🗃️ Database Tables

- Cities
- Customers
- Products
- Sales

---

## 📊 Business Questions Solved

### Easy
- Estimate coffee consumers in each city.
- Calculate total revenue in Q4 2023.
- Count product-wise sales.
- Find average sales per customer.
- Compare city population with estimated coffee consumers.

### Medium
- Top 3 selling products in each city.
- Customer segmentation by city.
- Compare average sales and rent per customer.

### Advanced
- Monthly sales growth analysis.
- Market potential analysis.
- Recommend top three expansion cities.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Pune generated the highest revenue.
- Delhi has the highest estimated coffee consumers.
- Jaipur recorded the highest number of customers.
- Pune and Jaipur offer lower rental costs per customer.
- Monthly sales trend indicates strong business growth.

---

## ✅ Final Recommendation

| City | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Pune | Highest revenue with low rent per customer |
| Delhi | Largest coffee consumer market |
| Jaipur | Highest customer base with affordable rent |
